Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 July 2026 · 最後更新：2026 年 7 月 27 日

English

BabyWise (“we”, “the app”) is a local-first pregnancy companion. This policy explains what we do and do not collect when you use https://babywise.pages.dev and related preview URLs.

English

1. Local-first data

Your pregnancy profile, calendar events, medicine logs, readings, Ask history, language, and settings are stored only in this browser’s local storage on your device. We do not operate a BabyWise user account database or cloud sync of your personal health journal.

  • No login is required for calendar and week tracking.
  • You can erase local data anytime with “Clean local data” in Settings, or by clearing this site’s data in your browser.

English

2. Ask (AI providers)

When you use Ask, the question, any optional context you include, and any photo you attach are sent to our Worker, which calls the AI provider you chose in Settings (for example Gemini, OpenAI, Grok, or Claude) with a server-side API key. You do not paste an API key in the app. Photos are compressed on your device before upload and are not stored as a server-side medical record.

  • We do not store Ask chats, photos, or your calendar as a server-side medical record.
  • The chosen provider processes the prompt (and image, if any) under that provider’s terms; usage counts against the app operator’s project quota.

English

3. Hosting and analytics

The app is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like most websites, hosting may log standard request metadata (for example IP address, URL, user agent) for security and operations. That is not the same as storing your calendar or pregnancy notes.

We use Google Tag Manager (container ID GTM-WFQ3VHZM) for product analytics such as anonymous page views and feature usage. Tags are designed to measure the product, not your due date, medicine names, calendar contents, or Ask questions.

English

4. Medical disclaimer

BabyWise is not medical advice. Ask results and any AI-generated text can be wrong. Always confirm with a qualified clinician.

English

5. Contact

For privacy questions about this app, open an issue on the project repository associated with your deployment, or contact the site operator who published this instance.

繁體中文

1. 本機優先資料

你的孕期檔案、日曆事項、用藥紀錄、數值、詢問歷史、語言與設定，只存在此瀏覽器的本機儲存。我們不營運 BabyWise 使用者帳號資料庫，也不雲端同步你的個人健康日記。

繁體中文

2. 詢問（AI 服務）

使用「詢問」時，問題、你勾選的可選脈絡，以及你附上的照片，會送到我們的 Worker，由伺服器端 API 金鑰呼叫你在設定中選擇的 AI（例如 Gemini、OpenAI、Grok 或 Claude）。你不必在 App 貼上 API 金鑰。照片會先在你的裝置壓縮再上傳，不會當成伺服器端病歷存檔。

  • 我們不會把詢問對話、照片或日曆當成伺服器端病歷存檔。
  • 所選服務商依其條款處理提示詞（及照片，如有）；用量計入營運者專案配額。

繁體中文

3. 託管與分析

應用託管於 Cloudflare Pages。與多數網站相同，託管端可能記錄一般請求中繼資料（例如 IP、網址、瀏覽器字串）以供安全與營運。這與儲存你的日曆或孕期備註不同。

我們使用 Google Tag Manager（容器 ID：GTM-WFQ3VHZM）了解產品使用情況（例如匿名瀏覽量、功能使用）。標籤設計以產品為主，不會把預產期、藥名、日曆內容或詢問文字送進分析。
