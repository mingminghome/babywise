BabyWise

BabyWise is a free, local-first pregnancy companion web app. It helps you track pregnancy weeks, keep a calendar of medicines and appointments, log simple readings, and optionally use Sign in with Google and use the in-app Ask feature for pregnancy-safety style guidance via free-tier Gemini (not medical advice).

Application purpose

The purpose of the BabyWise application is to help pregnant people track pregnancy progress and manage medicines and appointments on their own device. The product name is BabyWise.

What BabyWise does

  • Pregnancy week and day tracking from your dates
  • Calendar for medicines, appointments, and notes
  • Ask uses free-tier Gemini on our server (no login or API key paste)
  • Local-first privacy: profile, calendar, and logs stay in this browser

Why we request Google access

Ask uses free-tier Gemini on our server. We do not store your pregnancy calendar or medicine list in a server database. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
